Good to know

Clear expectations from the start.

Can you look after a website you did not build?

Yes, subject to a review of the website, its platform and the access available. Some sites may need preliminary repairs before ongoing care can begin.

Is every change included?

The subscription covers the maintenance and support agreed for your site. Larger redesigns, new features and substantial content work can be quoted separately.

Is security guaranteed?

No website service can promise that a site will never be attacked or experience downtime. The service focuses on sensible security upkeep, checks and practical help when an issue arises.

When does the subscription start?

After we review your website, agree the monthly fee and scope, and confirm the start date with you.
